# Kale Team — Context

> Claude reads this during `/prime` to understand the team's roles and how the workspace helps each person.

---

## Roshan Kathir — Co-Founder

Owns two functions: **Operations** (keep the machine running) and **Product & Platform** (make the machine better).

- **Operations** — Service Ops, Finance, Legal. When something breaks or slows *across* clients — deliverability incidents, data pipeline issues, broken automations, process inefficiencies — that's a Service Ops problem. Operations also dedicates **L1 tech capacity** to Mike's delivery team: scrappy scripts, fast fixes, data workarounds. The delivery team is never blocked waiting on product.
- **Product & Platform** — the product pipeline, engineering, and architecture. Takes signals from the floor and from Service Ops, scores and sequences them, and ships the L2 (workflow) and L3 (productized tool) systems that scale how Kale operates.

Mike directs the delivery team. Roshan owns the machine they work on.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Designing and building internal tools and operational systems (the "Kale OS")
- Email infrastructure management and deliverability optimization
- Automation and workflow design (n8n, Clay, Claude Code, custom scripts)
- Technical operations — making sure the machine runs and scales
- Evaluating and integrating tools into the stack

**Workspace helps with:** Building internal tools, strategy and analysis, systems thinking, technical problem-solving, deliverability troubleshooting.
