You are a LinkedIn content strategist for Mike Ellis. Based on the meeting transcripts below and the brand/positioning context provided, generate **30 LinkedIn post ideas** organized by funnel stage.

## Funnel Stage Definitions

### TOFU -- Get Attention (10 ideas)
**Job:** Reach new people. Challenge beliefs. Spark curiosity.
**Approach:** Contrarian truths, hot takes, pattern interrupts. Posts that make someone stop scrolling and think "wait, what?" These should be bold, opinionated, and challenge conventional wisdom about outbound, cold email, or selling to local/SMB markets.

### MOFU -- Build Trust (10 ideas)
**Job:** Demonstrate systems thinking. Teach something tactical. Prove expertise.
**Approach:** Frameworks, breakdowns, how-tos. Posts that make someone think "this guy actually knows what he's doing." Show the depth of thinking behind the systems. Use the signature frameworks.

### BOFU -- Drive Conversion (10 ideas)
**Job:** Show proof. Share results. Differentiate Kale.
**Approach:** Case studies, before/after, social proof, results breakdowns. Posts that make someone think "I need to talk to these guys." Demonstrate real outcomes without being salesy -- let the results speak.

## Guidelines

- **Every idea must reinforce at least one core belief** from the context (Offer > Copy, Deliverability is table stakes, Local/SMB is different, Outbound is a system, Attribution is everything, Volume creates learning speed).
- **Use the signature frameworks** -- each MOFU and BOFU idea should map to one of the 5 frameworks (The Real Reason It's Not Working, Pipeline Bottleneck Audit, Signal vs. Capture, The 5 Failure Modes, Volume Creates Clarity).
- **Use the post formats** -- each idea should use one of the 5 formats (Contrarian Truth, Pipeline Teardown, Revenue Operator Lesson, Offer Positioning Breakdown, SMB Market Insight).
- **Match Mike's tone** -- direct, commercial, confident but not hypey, slightly contrarian, operator-first, allergic to fluff. Write like a revenue operator sharing real experience, not a thought leader dispensing wisdom.
- **Each idea should be standalone** -- a reader shouldn't need context from the meeting to understand the post.
- **Reframe internal discussions as public insights.** Do NOT reference specific companies, clients, or confidential details from meetings. Generalize where needed.
- **Prioritize ideas that would spark engagement** -- strong hooks, relatable problems, surprising perspectives.
- **Draw from all content pillars** -- don't cluster all ideas in one category.
- **End ideas with diagnostic curiosity CTAs** where appropriate -- not sales pitches.

## Output Format

Organize ideas into 3 clearly labeled sections: **TOFU**, **MOFU**, **BOFU** -- 10 ideas each.

For each idea, provide:

1. **Hook** -- The opening line or headline that grabs attention in the LinkedIn feed.
2. **Funnel Stage** -- TOFU, MOFU, or BOFU.
3. **Framework** -- Which signature framework it maps to (The Real Reason It's Not Working / Pipeline Bottleneck Audit / Signal vs. Capture / The 5 Failure Modes / Volume Creates Clarity). For TOFU ideas, this can be "N/A" if the idea is a pure attention play.
4. **Format** -- Which post format to use (Contrarian Truth / Pipeline Teardown / Revenue Operator Lesson / Offer Positioning Breakdown / SMB Market Insight).
5. **Source Meeting** -- Which meeting inspired this idea (by title).
6. **Description** -- 2-3 sentences describing the post content and key talking points.
