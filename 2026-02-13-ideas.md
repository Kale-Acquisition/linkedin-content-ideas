## 2. 3 Scheduling Mistakes Killing Your Cold Email Reply Rates

**Type:** Tactical Tip  
**Format:** Text post  
**Source meeting:** Weekly Client Review  

We discovered campaigns weren't sending on weekends. Simple fix, massive impact. Here are 3 scheduling mistakes that tank cold email performance:

1. **Skipping weekends** — Decision-makers check email Saturday morning. You're missing prime real estate.
2. **Sending only M-F 9-5** — Everyone else does this. Stand out by hitting off-hours.
3. **Ignoring time zones** — If you're targeting local businesses, send when *they're* online, not when you're at your desk.

We adjusted one client's schedule to include weekend sends. Reply rates jumped because we weren't competing with Monday's inbox avalanche.
